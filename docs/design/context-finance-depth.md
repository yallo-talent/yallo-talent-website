## 2. The nine functions

### 2.1 Core banking platform

**Tools.** Temenos Transact · Temenos Infinity · Oracle FLEXCUBE Universal Banking · Oracle Banking Digital Experience · Infosys Finacle Core Banking · Finacle Digital Engagement Suite

**Roles.** Temenos Transact Consultant · T24 Technical Developer · Temenos Solution Architect · FLEXCUBE Functional Consultant · FLEXCUBE Technical Consultant · Finacle Functional Consultant · Finacle Technical Developer · Core Banking Solution Architect · Core Banking Business Analyst

### 2.2 Islamic banking

**This is the function that earns the page its region.** A UK-origin competitor cannot staff it credibly, and every major Middle East market has both fully Islamic banks and conventional banks running Islamic windows. It is first among equals here even though core banking is larger.

**Tools.** Temenos Islamic Banking · FLEXCUBE Islamic Banking · Finacle Islamic Banking

**Roles.** Islamic Banking Functional Consultant · Sharia-compliant Product Configuration Specialist · Islamic Treasury Consultant · Islamic Banking Business Analyst · Islamic Finance Product Owner

**Handle with care.** Describe what Yallo places and screens for. Do not explain Sharia compliance, do not characterise any ruling or standard, and do not imply Yallo certifies anything.

### 2.3 Digital channels and onboarding

**Tools.** Temenos Infinity · Oracle Banking Digital Experience · Finacle Digital Engagement Suite

**Roles.** Digital Banking Consultant · Customer Onboarding and eKYC Consultant · Mobile Banking Product Owner · Open Banking and API Consultant · Front-end Engineer, banking channels · Digital Channels Business Analyst

**One proposal, not ratified:** Backbase is genuinely widespread across Middle East banks and would sit here naturally. It is outside the five platforms Sumeet named, so it is **not** included. Flag it in the relay for his nod rather than adding it.

### 2.4 Payments and ISO 20022

**Tools.** Temenos Payments · Oracle Banking Payments · Finacle Payments · SWIFT and ISO 20022 message standards · the regional instant-payment schemes, named only: Aani in the UAE, sarie in Saudi Arabia, and Buna regionally

**Roles.** Payments Solution Architect · ISO 20022 Migration Consultant · SWIFT Consultant · Payments Integration Developer · Instant Payments Scheme Consultant · Payments Business Analyst · Payments Testing Specialist

**Name the schemes, never interpret them.** No mandate dates, no compliance deadlines, no statement of what any scheme obliges a bank to do. This is the same rule already applied to the regional security frameworks on the Cybersecurity & Risk desk.

### 2.5 Treasury and capital markets

**Tools.** Murex MX.3, across front office, market risk, collateral and XVA · Oracle Banking Treasury Management

**Roles.** Murex Front Office Consultant · Murex Back Office Consultant · Murex MxML Developer · Murex Datamart Developer · Murex Market Risk Consultant · Collateral and XVA Consultant · Treasury Business Analyst

### 2.6 Risk, regulatory reporting and financial crime

**Tools.** Oracle Financial Services Analytical Applications · anti-money-laundering and sanctions screening platforms · regulatory reporting to the region's central banks, named only · Basel and IFRS 9 frameworks, named only

**Roles.** IFRS 9 Consultant · Basel Reporting Consultant · OFSAA Consultant · AML and Sanctions Screening Consultant · Financial Crime Analyst · Regulatory Reporting Business Analyst · Model Validation Specialist

**Same naming discipline.** Regulators and frameworks are named as things a role is screened against. No obligation is described, no deadline given, no certification implied.
