## 1. Shape and depth

Eight functions, matching the finance approach rather than retail's twenty. Weighted to what Middle East universities and school groups actually run, because that is where the demand is: Vision 2030 education programmes in Saudi Arabia, the federal and emirate-level institutions in the UAE, and the international branch campuses across both.

Everything below is real market vocabulary: products as their vendors publish them, job titles as the market advertises them. **No figure, client, quotation, date or metric appears here and none may be added.** No claim about any ministry, regulator or accreditation body beyond naming it.

**The buyer.** A university CIO or programme director, or the IT lead of a school group. Not a student and not an academic. The page sells staffing for institutional systems programmes, in the same voice as every other L1: what Yallo places, screens and staffs.

**Tools.** research administration and grants management platforms · institutional repositories · research data management

**Named generically on purpose.** The vendor set here is fragmented and regionally variable, so naming specific products without evidence would be invention. If the repo carries evidence of a named platform, add it; otherwise leave it as written and flag it.

### 2.5 Institutional finance, HR and procurement

The enterprise back office of an institution, and where this L1 joins the platform desks rather than restating them.

**Tools.** Oracle Fusion · SAP · Workday · Microsoft Dynamics 365

**Roles.** These are the platform desks' roles applied to an institutional context, so **cross-link rather than duplicate**. One line, then a route to `/platforms/oracle`, `/platforms/workday` and `/platforms/microsoft`. Do not restate a role list that already lives there.

## 3. What makes this page not generic

Three things, and they should be visible in the copy rather than asserted:

1. **The academic calendar is the constraint.** A university cannot move go-live: registration and term start are fixed. That changes how a programme is staffed and it is the sentence a university CIO will recognise.
2. **Student records are the most sensitive migration in the sector.** Historical transcripts have no acceptable error rate, which is why §2.8 is a function rather than a footnote.
3. **The region is building institutions, not just upgrading them.** New campuses and new programmes need systems standing up from nothing, which is a different staffing shape from a replacement.

None of these needs a figure. Do not add one.
