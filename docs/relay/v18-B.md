## 2. What was found and fixed — the two that matter most

Everything is in the findings log; two are worth surfacing directly
because they are visible, real content changes on a live site, not
internal refinements:

**`/leadership` named the founder specifically** ("Sumeet Goenka, Founder
& CEO") against a photograph-styled card, and gave five more team members
initials monograms plus specific unverifiable career claims ("20+ years of
SAP delivery...", "Ex-Oracle Fusion delivery leader..."). This directly
contradicted round 10's own explicit ruling for this page: no ratified
named consultants exist, ship roles with credentials, no names, no
photographs. Rewrote to the pattern already live and unchallenged on
`/why-yallo`'s credentials section — role + what the role does, no
individual named. Also removed the same pattern (naming the founder) from
`/about`'s founding timeline, for consistency — same underlying fact
pattern, different page. **Nothing was invented to replace what was
removed**: the Richemont/Landmark/Alshaya EMEA company history was kept
only because it's already asserted consistently elsewhere on the site;
every specific tenure/employer claim for the other five roles was dropped
rather than replaced.

**`/jobs` hardcoded six specific "live" job openings** — named roles,
cities, durations ("12-month engagement", "Immediate start") — introduced
as "a sample of the enterprise programmes we're actively staffing."
`context-round10-scope.md` §7 lists the Volcanic `/jobs` punchout as a
not-yet-built cutover dependency, so there is no ATS behind this page and
these listings could not be real. Replaced with the six real, ratified
specialist desks (reusing the homepage's own `desks` export, not
hand-typed again) and an honest statement that there's no live jobs board
yet.

Both read as placeholder content from an earlier pass that was never
swept before publish review. Flagging both explicitly because they're the
kind of change that's worth a direct look rather than trusting the diff.

## 8. Notes for Session A

- **`EditorialLayout.module.css`'s `.sectionAlt` composes `band-dark`** —
  a *permanently* dark surface, identical in both themes — for the
  differentiators/credentials sections on `/why-yallo` (and, by the same
  shared component, potentially `/about`, `/leadership`, `/jobs`,
  `/insights`). In **light** theme this alternates correctly against the
  hero/CTA's own `band-dark`. In **dark** theme, the plain `.section`'s
  inherited page background resolves to the *same* colour as
  `.sectionAlt`'s permanent dark, so all mid-page sections collapse into
  one undifferentiated dark surface with no rhythm — confirmed by
  computed style, not assumed. Not a contrast failure (text stays
  legible), a design-rhythm one. Logged in full, `docs/design/
  context-round10-findings.md` §2 finding 3. **Exact ask: should
  `.sectionAlt`'s mid-page use compose `band-invert` instead of
  `band-dark`, reserving `band-dark` for `.hero`/`.bottomCta` only?**
- **`LegalPageShell.module.css`'s `.body` composes `band-dark`** too,
  making `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/cookies` permanently dark end to end —
  correctly implemented, not a bug, but disagrees with
  `/case-studies/[slug]`'s light register for the same "Read mode"
  content class. §9 finding 3, same file.
- Both of the above were **logged, not touched** — `src/components/**`
  and `src/styles/**` are your territory per §4. I only edited
