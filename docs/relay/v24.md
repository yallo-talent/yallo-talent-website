## 7. Risks

- **The PDF is committed, not built in CI.** Change a figure in the extract and the pages move; the PDF does not, until someone runs `pnpm research:pdf` against a running server. `check:research-dataset` does not catch this. **A CI step that regenerates and diffs the PDF is the proper fix and is not built.**
