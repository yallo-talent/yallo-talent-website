## 1. What this is

Every Talent Pool Report's Company tab lists the top employers of that platform's talent pool in the
region. A company whose staff carry SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, cloud, data or AI titles is, by construction,
already running that platform — which makes it a qualified prospect for Yallo Talent outbound before a
single call is made. This is standard business intelligence about who buys what, not a LinkedIn-derived
statistic, and it is not covered by the rank-not-republish rule that governs the Blueprint.

**One caution that does apply:** do not represent any line below as "LinkedIn says X hires this" in
external-facing material. Verify independently before naming a prospect in outbound material that could
be traced back to a scraped source.
