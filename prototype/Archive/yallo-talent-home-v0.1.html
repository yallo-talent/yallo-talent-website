The commitment

Three things we put in writing.

01 · Speed

Shortlist in 72 hours, or you hear why on day one.

Three architect-screened candidates within 72 hours of a complete brief. Where the role cannot be filled to that bar in that window, we tell you at the start and give the market reason, rather than sending volume to look busy.

02 · Transparency

You see the screen, not just the shortlist.

Every candidate arrives with the architect's screening notes attached, and with the reasons the others were rejected. You are buying a judgement, so you get to inspect it.

03 · Risk

The first ten working days are at our risk.

If a contractor we place does not meet the brief within ten working days, we replace them and you do not pay for those days.

Full terms apply per engagement and are set out in the contract. contract review needed before publish
