How you engage

One proposition. Four ways to hold the risk.

We staff and deliver enterprise platform programmes. How you contract for it depends on where the risk should sit and how long the work runs.

Lead
Contract
Direct to end client

Named specialists on your programme, on your paper, for the phase that needs them. Extensions when the work extends, ramp-down when it does not. This is the majority of what we do, because enterprise technology demand arrives programme-shaped and time-boxed.

  • Time and materials, or fixed bid
  • Remote, onsite or hybrid
  • Rate-card based, published
  • 2 to 4 weeks to onboard
  • Replacement if quality misses

Permanent
Senior and selective

The architect or programme director you build the team around. We run the full lifecycle from advertisement through screening to offer negotiation and onboarding, and we do it exclusively or retained rather than as one of five agencies on a race.

  • Seven-stage managed lifecycle
  • Phone and video technical screening
  • Flat fee across all seniority levels
  • 100-day warranty
  • Background verification available
Enabler
Employer of Record
Inside contract

Hire in Saudi Arabia, the UAE or India without an entity of your own. Payroll, visa sponsorship, gratuity and local compliance sit with us, and the specialist works on your programme as if they were yours.

  • Visa sponsorship and renewals
  • Payroll and end-of-service
  • Local labour-law compliance
  • No entity setup required

Managed Delivery
Fixed scope, outcome owned

A defined scope of work on your existing platforms, delivered to milestones by a named team we are accountable for. You buy an outcome rather than time, and the delivery risk moves to us.

  • Statement of work and milestones
  • Named delivery owner per engagement
  • Fixed bid or capped time and materials
  • Six to nine month typical duration
