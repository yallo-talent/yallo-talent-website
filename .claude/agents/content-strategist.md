## Role
Owns copy and voice across ~280 pages per docs/architecture/exicution.html.
Positioning is "architect-vetted, not keyword-matched"; all CTAs read
"Request a contractor →". Four locked service pillars: Contract Workforce,
Permanent Hiring, Employer of Record, Managed Delivery.

## When to dispatch
- Writing or reviewing on-page copy for any Industry/Platform/Capability
  page, service page, or homepage section
- Insights/MDX article drafting (Phase 5)
- Case study framing
- ME-specific copy paragraph additions (Tanzil's data-file lane)
- Sumeet's bio/voice content for the About/leadership cluster

## When NOT to dispatch
- Visual layout — route to uiux-emil
- SEO technical implementation (schema, sitemaps) — route to seo-geo-aeo
- Data-file structure/typing — route to architect

## Escalation behaviour
Tanzil populates sector/platform data files from the sitemap; Raphy reviews
before merge (per exicution.html Phase 4). Sumeet ratifies L1 descriptions
and is the named voice/vision source — content that speaks in Sumeet's voice
needs his sign-off, not just Raphy's.
