### Needed Rapid Onboarding and Expert Azure Data Engineers

Alshaya Group, A leading international retail enterprise faced the challenge of building a strategic Azure data platform. To meet their digital transformation goals, they needed to rapidly onboard skilled Azure Data Engineers and scale resources dynamically. Without the right talent, their data strategy risked delays and inefficiencies.

## The Challenge

### Urgent Need for Skilled Azure Data Engineers

Alshaya Group embarked on a major initiative to develop a new data platform on Azure, their strategic cloud platform. Ensuring the success of this critical project required a team of highly skilled Azure Data Engineers capable of delivering results from day one.

Key challenges included:

- Quick Onboarding: Resources had to be deployed rapidly to meet tight deadlines.
- Scalability: The ability to scale the team size based on project phases was essential.
- Expertise in Azure Data Architecture: Experienced professionals were needed to deliver a robust and scalable data architecture.

An efficient solution was necessary to address these challenges and ensure long-term project success.

## How YALLO Helped

### TS/EA as Service and Talent in a Box Solutions

YALLO provided a customised solution combining strategic advisory and agile talent acquisition to address the organisation’s needs:

- Dedicated Azure Data Engineering Team: Using the Talent in a Box approach, YALLO quickly assembled a team of experienced Azure Data Engineers, perfectly aligned with the organisation’s technical and cultural requirements.
- Enterprise Architecture Expertise: Through our EA as-a-Service model, YALLO ensured the platform was built on a robust Azure data architecture, meeting scalability and performance demands.
- Rapid Onboarding: The streamlined onboarding process enabled engineers to contribute immediately.
- Flexible Resource Scaling: YALLO provided the flexibility to seamlessly scale the team size, optimising resources at every stage of the project.

This approach equipped the organisation with the talent and technical direction needed to effectively build and maintain their Azure data platform.

## The Result

### Client Confidence and Program Expansion

YALLO’s tailored solution delivered exceptional outcomes:

- On-Time Delivery: Rapid onboarding ensured the project met critical deadlines.
- Scalable Team Dynamics: Flexibility to ramp resources up or down optimised costs and maintained efficiency.
- High Client Trust: The success of the initial phase led to team expansion, reinforcing YALLO’s position as a trusted partner.

This collaboration highlights YALLO’s ability to deliver high-performing teams tailored to the unique demands of fast-paced, high-stakes environments.
