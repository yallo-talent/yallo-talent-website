## Client Context

Majid Al Futtaim (MAF) is one of the Middle East’s largest diversified enterprises, operating a broad portfolio of enterprise systems supporting finance, procurement, cloud, and customer operations.

## Business Objectives & Challenges

To support ongoing business and technology initiatives, MAF aimed to improve cost efficiency, delivery quality, and governance across its Time & Materials (T&M) support model. Reliance on multiple vendors across platforms such as SAP, Oracle, Hyperion, Azure, Salesforce, and Microsoft led to rising costs, inconsistent consultant quality, slow onboarding, and fragmented accountability.

## YALLO’s Role

YALLO partnered with MAF as a unified delivery partner, consolidating cross-platform talent delivery into a single, governed model that improved execution quality and predictability.

- Consolidated multi-vendor support into a single delivery model covering ERP, cloud, finance, procurement, and CRM platforms
- Deployed project-aligned teams to ensure continuity, accountability, and consistent delivery standards
- Implemented a hybrid onsite and remote delivery approach to balance cost and responsiveness
- Enabled predictable onboarding through a scalable and reliable delivery framework
## Outcome

- Improved cost efficiency across enterprise technology support
- More consistent consultant quality and delivery reliability
- Faster onboarding and improved resource predictability
- Simplified vendor management with clearer ownership and governance
