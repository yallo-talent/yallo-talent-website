## Client Context

A large enterprise undertook an Oracle Fusion Financials transformation with a focus on Fixed Assets (FA) to improve asset governance, financial control, and reporting accuracy. The programme involved migrating complex asset data from legacy systems while ensuring compliance and seamless integration with the General Ledger.

## Business Objectives & Challenges

As part of the finance modernisation initiative, the organisation needed to:

- Implement Oracle Fusion Fixed Assets across diverse asset type
- Manage the full asset lifecycle, including Capitalised, Expensed, CIP, Leased, and Group Assets
- Define consistent asset categories, depreciation methods, and asset books
- Migrate high-volume legacy asset data without financial discrepancies
- Reconcile asset balances, depreciation, and accumulated depreciation with GL
- Maintain stable post-go-live operations alongside enhancements
## YALLO’s Role

YALLO partnered with the client as an implementation and support enablement partner, providing structured delivery support and deep Oracle Fusion Fixed Assets expertise.

YALLO supported FA configuration and lifecycle transactions, defined depreciation structures and asset books, enabled accurate data migration and validation, reconciled FA and GL balances, and provided post-go-live support to stabilise operations and address enhancements.

## Outcome

- Successful Oracle Fusion Fixed Assets implementation
- Accurate migration and reconciliation of asset data
- Improved asset governance and financial controls
- Reduced post-go-live depreciation issues
