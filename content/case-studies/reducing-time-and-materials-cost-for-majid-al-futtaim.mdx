### They Faced High Support Costs, Inconsistent Talent, and Delayed Onboarding

Majid Al Futtaim (MAF), one of the Middle East’s largest and most diverse enterprises, faced mounting challenges in managing their technology support operations. With critical systems spanning SAP S/4HANA, Oracle Fusion, Hyperion, Coupa, Ariba, Azure, Salesforce, and Microsoft Dynamics 365, the organisation relied on multiple vendors to provide skilled consultants across these platforms.

However, this fragmented model resulted in:

- Rising T&M costs
- Inconsistent consultant quality
- Significant onboarding delays
- Difficulty maintaining continuity and accountability

These inefficiencies slowed down projects, impacted service quality, and made resource planning increasingly difficult. MAF needed a partner capable of delivering high-quality talent across all platforms while reducing cost and increasing predictability.

## The Challenge

### High Costs, Low Quality, and Multi-Vendor Inefficiencies

The organisation struggled to maintain operational stability due to:

- Escalating support team costs driven by vendor inconsistencies
- Variable consultant quality, affecting project outcomes and system reliability
- Slow onboarding cycles, delaying critical work and causing project overruns
- A multi-vendor environment that lacked standardisation, governance, and unified accountability
- Increasing demand for diverse technical skills across ERP, cloud, finance, procurement, and CRM systems

MAF required a single, reliable partner capable of supplying top-tier consultants across all technologies while helping reduce costs and improve delivery discipline.

## How YALLO Helped

### A Comprehensive Talent Solution with a Unified Vendor Model

YALLO stepped in as a strategic partner, delivering a holistic solution designed to streamline talent operations across the entire technology ecosystem.

### 1. A Single Vendor Covering All Technical Platforms

YALLO became the only partner capable of supplying talent across Oracle, SAP, Hyperion, Azure, Salesforce, and other key systems—eliminating the need for multiple vendors and reducing administrative burden.

### 2. Robust Project-Aligned Delivery Teams

MAF benefited from teams of consultants aligned specifically to their project and operational needs, ensuring continuity, accountability, and consistent quality.

### 3. Hybrid Delivery Model (Remote + Onsite)

A flexible mix of onsite and remote consultants optimised both cost and availability, ensuring support coverage without unnecessary T&M spend.

### 4. Higher Quality, Lower Cost

Through rigorous vetting, domain expertise, and efficient delivery structures, YALLO significantly improved consultant quality while reducing overall support costs.

### 5. Predictable, Scalable Talent Supply

YALLO’s Talent Network ensured that MAF always had access to the right resources at the right time—minimising delays and accelerating onboarding.

This unified model improved operational efficiency and provided transparency, consistency, and control across all technology functions.

## The Result

### Preferred Vendor Status and Ongoing Expansion

YALLO’s model delivered measurable and lasting improvements:

- Reduced T&M support costs across multiple technology areas
- Consistently high consultant performance and reliability
- Faster onboarding and improved delivery predictability
- Streamlined vendor management with one trusted partner

MAF recognised the significant value generated and appointed YALLO as one of their preferred T&M vendors for technology talent solutions.The partnership continues to expand, driven by ongoing successful implementations and consistently high-quality support.
