## Business Objectives & Challenges

Chalhoub Group is a leading luxury retail and distribution group in the Middle East, operating complex supply chain environments that support multiple brands, markets, and growth initiatives.

As part of ongoing supply chain transformation initiatives, Chalhoub Group needed to quickly onboard experienced Supply Chain Architects and Business Analysts. The roles were critical to bridging strategy and execution, but the niche skill requirements and market conditions made timely hiring challenging, impacting project momentum.

## YALLO’s Role

YALLO partnered with Chalhoub Group to provide rapid, high-quality talent aligned to both technical requirements and organisational culture, enabling key initiatives to move forward without further delay.

- Identified and deployed experienced supply chain architects and business analysts within weeks
- Leveraged a hybrid delivery model combining UK-based remote expertise with onsite presence in the UAE
- Ensured strong alignment with Chalhoub Group’s technical, domain, and cultural expectations
- Provided a streamlined recruitment process to minimise disruption to ongoing programmes
## Outcome

- Critical roles filled within weeks, restoring project momentum
- High-quality consultants integrated quickly and delivered value from day one
- Improved confidence in delivery timelines for supply chain initiatives
- Expanded partnership as Chalhoub Group engaged YALLO for additional talent and support needs
