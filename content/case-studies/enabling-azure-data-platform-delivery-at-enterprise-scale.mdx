## Client Context

Alshaya Group is a large international retail enterprise undertaking a major data modernisation initiative to support analytics, scalability, and long-term digital transformation.

## Business Objectives & Challenges

To support its data strategy, Alshaya Group needed to rapidly build delivery capacity for a strategic Azure data platform. Key challenges included onboarding experienced Azure data engineering talent at speed, ensuring architectural robustness, and retaining flexibility to scale delivery resources in line with project phases.

## YALLO’s Role

YALLO partnered with Alshaya Group to enable end-to-end delivery of the Azure data platform by combining enterprise architecture leadership with agile, scalable engineering capability.

- Assembled a dedicated team of experienced Azure data engineers aligned to delivery requirements
- Provided enterprise architecture oversight to ensure a scalable, cloud-native data platform
- Enabled rapid onboarding so engineers contributed from day one
- Supported flexible team scaling to match programme milestones and evolving needs
## Outcome

- Delivery timelines met through fast mobilisation of specialist capability
- Robust Azure data architecture established to support current and future workloads
- Optimised cost and resource utilisation through scalable team structures
- Expanded engagement as YALLO continued supporting subsequent phases of the data platform initiative
