## Client Context

Al Tayer Group, a leading UAE-based conglomerate, embarked on a large-scale SAP S/4HANA transformation programme to modernise its enterprise systems. Wipro was appointed as the strategic System Integrator (SI) responsible for delivering the programme, requiring deep SAP expertise.

## Business Objectives & Challenges

To meet aggressive programme timelines, Wipro needed immediate access to highly specialised SAP S/4HANA talent capable of operating in a complex, client-facing environment. Key challenges included

- Urgent demand for niche SAP skills across SAP CAR, GRC, SAC, SD, MM, FICO, and Group Reporting
- Requirement for onsite deployment, combining technical depth with strong stakeholder engagement
- Ensuring delivery continuity and quality was essential to keeping the transformation on track.
## Yallo’s Role

Yallo partnered closely with Wipro to strengthen delivery capability for the Al Tayer programme by providing targeted, high-quality SAP talent.

## Outcome

- Rapidly deploying niche SAP S/4HANA consultants aligned to project requirements
- Ensuring all resources met technical, delivery, and client engagement expectations
- Actively quality-managing delivery performance through regular coordination.
- Maintaining continuous feedback loops to ensure team effectiveness.
