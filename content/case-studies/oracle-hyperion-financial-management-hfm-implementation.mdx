## Client Context

Majid Al Futtaim (MAF) is a leading diversified conglomerate operating across retail, real estate, leisure, and entertainment, with complex financial consolidation and reporting requirements across multiple geographies and entities.

## Business Objectives & Challenges

As part of its finance transformation roadmap, MAF aimed to strengthen governance, predictability, and delivery outcomes for its Oracle Hyperion Financial Management (HFM) implementation to support accurate group-wide reporting and compliance. Their key challenges were managing scale, complexity, and delivery alignment in a group-wide HFM programme

## Yallo’s Role

Yallo partnered with MAF to provide specialised Hyperion expertise, delivery leadership, and additional execution capacity, working closely with internal teams and existing implementation partners.

- Deployed senior HFM solution architecture and specialist functional/technical consultants
- Aligned the solution design with MAF’s consolidation and reporting requirements
- Collaborated with finance, IT, and business stakeholders to resolve dependencies and accelerate progress
- Introduced structured governance, phased delivery, and improved delivery transparency
## Outcome

- Successful first-phase go-live enabling accurate and timely financial consolidation
- Improved delivery predictability, governance, and programme visibility
- Clear roadmap established for subsequent phases
- Strengthened long-term partnership for future finance transformation initiatives
