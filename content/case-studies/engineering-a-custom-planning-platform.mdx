## Client Context

Alshaya Group is one of the region’s largest retail operators, managing complex planning operations across multiple brands, geographies, and high-volume retail environments.

## Business Objectives & Challenges

As Alshaya continued to scale its retail portfolio, its planning landscape needed to support increasing complexity, faster enhancement cycles, and long-term flexibility. Key challenges included managing total cost of ownership, enabling faster rollout of new capabilities across business units, and establishing stronger ownership over the planning technology roadmap.

## YALLO’s Role

YALLO partnered with Alshaya to design and deliver a modern, custom-built planning platform, providing enterprise architecture leadership, dedicated engineering capacity, and end-to-end delivery support in close collaboration with internal business and technology teams.

- Designed a scalable, cloud-ready enterprise architecture supporting Forecasting, Allocation, and Replenishment workflows
- Deployed a dedicated cross-functional engineering team, including full-stack, data, and UX specialists
- Worked in an agile, co-creation model with Alshaya stakeholders to align functional requirements, data models, and user experience
- Delivered the first phase of the platform at enterprise scale, meeting agreed performance and quality benchmarks
- Established a phased transition approach to ensure continuity and minimise operational risk
## Outcome

- Significant reduction in total cost of ownership through increased platform ownership and flexibility
- Strong internal adoption across business and technology teams
- Clear, co-created roadmap defined to scale the platform across the wider retail portfolio
