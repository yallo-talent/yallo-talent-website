## Client Context

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) was delivering a large-scale Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) integration program for an enterprise customer in Saudi Arabia. The program involved complex SOA-based integrations across multiple internal and external systems, with strict security, governance, and delivery timelines.

## Business Objectives & Challenges

The engagement required stabilizing and accelerating delivery across a high-complexity Oracle EBS SOA landscape.

- Multiple integrations using SOAP, REST, XML-based services, and middleware adapters
- Secure communication using SSL, certificates, and keystores
- Dependency management across DB, JMS, FTP, LDAP, and REST interfaces
- Need for clear Fit-Gap analysis and alignment with business requirements
- Pressure to progress SIT, UAT, and production readiness without delivery slippage
## YALLO’s Role

YALLO partnered with TCS as an implementation support and delivery enablement partner, embedded into the program to strengthen execution and governance. Key contributions included.

- Supporting Oracle EBS SOA integration design and validation
- Guiding secure integration practices (SSL, certificates, keystores)
- Assisting Fit-Gap analysis and functional requirement alignment
- Strengthening documentation across solution design, SIT/UAT test scripts, and training materials
## Outcome

- Improved stability and reliability of Oracle EBS integrations
- Reduced delivery risk across SIT and UAT phases
- Better governance, documentation quality, and delivery transparency
- Clear foundation established for production support and future enhancements
