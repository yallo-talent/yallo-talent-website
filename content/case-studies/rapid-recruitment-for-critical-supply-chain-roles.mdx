### They Faced Challenges in Filling Critical Supply Chain Roles

Chalhoub Group, A leading luxury retail distributor faced challenges in filling critical Supply Chain Architect and Business Analyst roles. The lack of experienced talent stalled progress on key initiatives, creating a gap between strategy and execution.

## The Challenge

### Talent Gaps in Key Supply Chain Roles

Chalhoub Group encountered significant difficulties recruiting experienced professionals for vital supply chain roles. Despite months of working with other talent agencies, they struggled to find candidates who met their specific requirements.

These delays disrupted project timelines and hindered supply chain optimisation efforts. In search of a swift and reliable solution, they turned to YALLO.

## How YALLO Helped

### Swift Talent Acquisition and Tailored Solutions

To support Chalhoub Group, YALLO leveraged its expertise in talent strategy and deep understanding of the retail supply chain domain to deliver value:

- Rapid Recruitment: Within 2-3 weeks, YALLO successfully filled both roles with hand-picked consultants possessing the right skills and experience.
- Hybrid Team Model: A hybrid team was formed, with consultants based remotely in the UK and onsite at the organisation’s headquarters in the UAE.
- Tailored Talent Matching: YALLO ensured the consultants were a perfect fit, aligning with both technical and cultural requirements.
- Proactive Approach: The efficient recruitment process eliminated delays, setting a benchmark for swift and effective talent acquisition.

This approach enabled the organisation to resume its supply chain transformation projects confidently, knowing the right professionals were driving their initiatives.
